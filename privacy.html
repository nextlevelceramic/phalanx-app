Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026.

PHALANX is a private assistant for one person. Only the account phalanxactual@gmail.com signs in to it.

What it uses

With permission from that account, PHALANX reads, sends and organizes its own Gmail messages, and reads and writes its own Google Calendar events and Google Drive files.

Where data goes

Data from Google stays on the owner's own computer. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. It is not used to train general AI models.

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The account owner can remove access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
